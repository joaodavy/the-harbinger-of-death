João Davi de Lima e Silva

João Davi is a Brazilian writer who... Ok, ok, I won't talk about myself in the third person anymore, I promise.

Right now, I'm sixteen years old, the same age I was when the book was published.

The thing is, I didn't actually finish the book at this age.

Actually, I was fifteen, the same age I was when I started writing the book.

Well... we all know publishing a book is not that easy, so I had to wait a few months for it to be ready. Now, I'm sixteen.

About me... I can tell you some things, but I will focus more on my literary, philosophical and psychological influences.

My reason for that is: These people shaped so much of my thinking (therefore, my writing) that I have to talk about them

Literature: Dostoevsky, J.R.R Tolkien.

Philosophy: Kierkegaard, Camus.

Psychology: Carl Jung, Sigmund Freud.

There are many others, but i decided to stick to only two of them in each category.

Oh, and feel free to discover traces of all of them in the book.

My friend, obviously I wouldn't explain it all for you, that would absolutely ruin your experience.

As for my language, I experimented a lot and had lots of fun doing that. Writing is great, I recommend it!

Reading, you will discover the results of my experiments.

The structuring, the thinking, the changing, the plotting, all the foreshadowing! I loved doing all that!

And talk about the themes, man! Talking about the things that anguish, disturb and fascinate me was wonderful.

I read a lot, I think a lot. Translating all that to my own book was unforgettable. Hope you'll enjoy my little book!

All the glory to God. Without Him, none of this would be possible.
