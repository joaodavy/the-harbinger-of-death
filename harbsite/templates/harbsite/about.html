Death's Favorite Son

What does he have left? Did he ever have anything at all? What changed?

This world is full of misery. For some, it is a nightmare; for others... it is more like a farm or a bank.

In the midst of all that, someone ended up losing. Losing everything.

So yes, he did have something, but... if he doesn't remember it, what good is it? Does it still matter?

Did he remain the same person? Well... if living like that is the same as being a person...

"The Harbinger of Death" ("O Arauto da Morte", in the original) is a book that explores many themes.

Guilt, loneliness, desperation, longing for... well, the end.

Sacrifice, choices, violence.

Pain, agony, transformation.

Identity, emptiness, loss.

Forgiveness, kindness, growth.

In a world full of despair, what would you choose to do?

What would you choose to be?
